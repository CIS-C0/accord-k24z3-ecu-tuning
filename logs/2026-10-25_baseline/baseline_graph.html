Overview

How it is read: decoded HDS .dat files (16-byte records: byte position, DID, value, time in ms). Scales checked against the HDS screen: rpm = raw / 4, ECT = raw − 40, throttle = raw / 200 °, ignition advance = (raw − 128) / 2. The pedal is shown as a percentage of the recorded maximum. The clutch is not logged; the gear is derived from the rpm / speed ratio.
